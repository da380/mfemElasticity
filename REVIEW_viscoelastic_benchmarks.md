## 3. Test fix: `tests/TestMeshManifest.cpp`

This was a pre-existing race under `ctest -j`. The MeshManifest tests
wrote and deleted the same fixed file names in the shared test
directory, so one test's clean-up could delete files another was
reading (one parallel failure seen). The `Files` fixture now writes into
a directory of its own, named after the running test, and removes it
afterwards. The file names inside are unchanged, because the manifest
resolves them relative to its own directory. Checked with 20 runs of the
five tests at `-j 8` in each build.

## 8. Findings worth your attention

1. **ExpTrap loses half an order on stiff multi-branch bodies under
   stress control** (order about 1.5 against SDIRK23's 2). It shows
   as time floors near 1e-4 in `prony_lid`/`burgers_lid` slabs and
   spheres, where SDIRK23 is up to 16× more accurate at the same
   step. It's not analysed; the hypothesis is stiff components in the
   strain within a step.
2. **Load jumps must be on the step grid.** Unaligned, every scheme's
   order collapses (0.2–0.7). Kinks only cap RK4 at order 2. The box
   driver aligns them by default.
3. **Material interfaces must lie on element faces** (now enforced).
   A misfit interface stalls the composite rheology and leaves the
   pointwise one at first order, and the error is invisible at t = 0+.
4. **Viscosity contrasts don't cost time accuracy, they cost CG.** CG
   iterations per solve go from about 55 to 150–340 (500–2500 with
   smooth depth grading) for the effective-modulus schemes; ETD1 and
   RK4, on the unrelaxed operator, stay flat. **This points at the
   preconditioner.**
5. **ETD1 doesn't converge when dt ≫ τ_min** on spatially structured
   problems (high-contrast slab, weak lateral region), though it does
   on the column.
6. **The adaptive trapezoid chases irrelevant fast transients:** 35k
   solves at τ = 1e-8, and runs of over an hour on the weak lateral
   disc. It needs a step floor or an observable-weighted estimate.
7. **The slab reference fails at contrasts ≥ 1e4.** AAA can't span
   that many decades of rate. It now checks itself and falls back to
   Talbot for Heaviside loads. The first contrast results had been
   scored against the broken modal sums and are corrected.
8. **Non-gravitating free bodies:** a Maxwell shell that relaxes fully
   around an elastic core leaves the core with a zero-stiffness mode
   (not removed by the whole-body rigid projector). Odd lateral
   variations force it, and the response diverged (C = 1e4). Hence
   `lateral_weak` uses an SLS mantle (μ∞ = 0.1). This matters for any
   future non-gravitating test with that geometry.
9. **Gaver–Stehfest:** on exact transforms, order 12 is good to
   1e-7…1.6e-4. Order 16 is no better: in double precision its gain
   on transform errors (1.3e9) outweighs the extra terms. The gain
   is 3.4e6 at order 12. Your order-16 check (moved the reference by
   less than 5e-4) bounds random noise in pyslfp's transforms to about
   1e-12, so the inversion is an unlikely cause of the degree-4 l′
   mismatch. Smooth systematic transform errors are not excluded.

## 9. Open points and decisions for you

- **Preconditioning of the effective operator** at high contrast (§8.4):
  options are BoomerAMG elasticity settings, or deflating the relaxed
  region's near-null space.
- **Adaptive stepping:** a lower step bound or a different estimator
  (§8.6).
- **Not done:** manufactured solutions with sideways viscosity
  variation in the box (sympy-generated forcing). The sphere lateral
  study covers lateral variation for the integrators, but against a
  step reference, not an exact one.
- **Server profile:** the 3-D p = 2 runs (≈1.5 h each on 4 ranks) and
  the 3-D lateral study have not been run.
- The degree-4 l′ question: a direct test would re-evaluate a few
  pyslfp transform values at tighter solver tolerances.

## 10. Reproducing

From `<build>/benchmarks/viscoelastic/box` and `.../sphere`:

```
./study steppers     # ~15 min, 5933 runs on one element
./study slab         # local profile
./study stehfest
./study contrast     # ~2 h on 10 cores
cd ../sphere
./study radial       # ~15 min
./study lateral      # ~1.5 h
```

Results and figures go to `runs/` under each. Everything here was run
with `--out runs` in `build_parallel`.
